Pastil

Pastil originated in Maguindanao, Mindanao, in the Philippines, as a traditional, portable halal meal created by the Maguindanaon people.

Tiyula Itlum

Tiyula Itlum is a traditional dish from Mindanao, known for its unique preparation and flavors that reflect the region's culinary heritage.

Mindanao

Chicken Piyanggang

Chicken piyanggang (piyanggang manok) comes from the Tausug people of the Sulu archipelago and Mindanao in the southern Philippines, including areas like Basilan and Tawi-Tawi.

Mindanao

Curacha with Alavar sauce

Curacha with Alavar sauce is a traditional dish from Mindanao, known for its unique blend of flavors and spices.

Mindanao

Singulaw

Singulaw is a popular Filipino fusion dish from Mindanao and the Visayas that combines smoky grilled pork belly (sinugba) with tangy, vinegar-cured raw fish ceviche (kinilaw). It is a traditional dish from Mindanao, known for its unique blend of flavors and spices.
