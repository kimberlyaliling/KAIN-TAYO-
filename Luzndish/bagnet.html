Ilocos Norte

Bagnet

Bagnet is a deep fried crispy pork belly dish that is similar to lechon kawali. It originated from Ilocos and is considered to be a top favorite among Filipinos. This is best served bagoong monamon. it is a dip made of fermented anchovies.

Gather

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram pork belly, skin on
  • 2 tablespoons salt
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • Water for simmering
  • Oil for deep frying

Make it

Recipe

  1. Place the pork belly in a pot with the salt, garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, and enough water to cover it.
  2. Simmer until the pork is tender, about 45 to 60 minutes. Remove it and pat the skin completely dry.
  3. Leave the pork uncovered in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, to dry the skin.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pot. Fry the pork over medium heat until the skin is crisp and golden.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, slice into pieces, and serve with sukang Iloko or tomato-onion dip.
