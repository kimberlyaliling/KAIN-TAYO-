Ilocos Norte

Ladek

Ladek is a traditional Ilocano dish originating from the northern part of the Philippines, specifically the Ilocos Region. Often compared to the famous Kapampangan Sisig, Ladek is primarily made from boiled pork head parts (such as the snout, cheeks, and ears) that are finely chopped and sautéed. It is known for its savory, tangy, and slightly spicy flavor profile, achieved by cooking the meat with indigenous cane vinegar (Sukang Ilocos), soy sauce, garlic, onions, and ginger. It is a highly popular pulutan (appetizer paired with cold drinks) but is equally enjoyed as a hearty, flavorful main dish served with warm steamed rice.

Gather

Ingredients

  • Pork face/mask (snout and ears), cleaned thoroughly
  • Water
  • Whole peppercorns and a pinch of salt (for the boiling broth)
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Cane vinegar (preferably Sukang Ilocos)
  • Soy sauce
  • Long green or red chili peppers (siling haba/siling labuyo)
  • Salt and ground black pepper
  • Cooking oil
  • Pork brain, boiled and mashed (optional, for authentic creaminess)

Make it

Recipe

  1. In a large pot, combine the pork mask, water, whole peppercorns, and a pinch of salt. Boil over medium heat until the meat is fork-tender.
  2. Remove the pork from the broth, let it cool completely, and chop it finely into small, bite-sized pieces..
  3. In a wide pan or wok, heat a small amount of cooking oil over medium heat. Sauté the garlic, onion, and ginger until aromatic and slightly softened.
  4. Add the finely chopped pork to the pan. Cook and stir continuously until the meat starts to brown, crisp up slightly, and release its own natural fats.
  5. Pour in the soy sauce and cane vinegar. Allow the mixture to simmer for a few minutes without stirring to cook off the strong, raw acidity of the vinegar.
  6. If using, toss in the mashed pork brain and mix thoroughly to coat the meat, which gives the dish a rich, creamy, and binded texture.
  7. Add the chopped chili peppers and season with salt and ground black pepper according to your preference.
  8. Continue to stir-fry for another 3 to 5 minutes until all the flavors are well combined and absorbed by the meat.
