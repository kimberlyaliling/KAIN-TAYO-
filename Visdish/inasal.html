Chicken Inasal

Chicken Inasal is a famous grilled chicken dish that originated in Bacolod City, Negros Occidental. Unlike standard barbecue that uses a soy sauce marinade, Inasal is marinated in a mixture of calamansi, pepper, coconut vinegar, and annatto (atsuete), giving it a distinct yellowish-orange color and a tangy, savory flavor. It is heavily basted with annatto oil while grilling over hot coals, which keeps the meat incredibly juicy and smoky. It is best enjoyed with garlic rice and a dipping sauce made of sinamak (spiced vinegar) and soy sauce.

Gather

Ingredients

  • Chicken quarters (leg and thigh)
  • Lemongrass (tanglad), pounded and chopped
  • Garlic, minced
  • Ginger, minced
  • Coconut vinegar (or cane vinegar)
  • Calamansi juice
  • Brown sugar
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Annatto oil (atsuete oil), for basting
  • Bamboo skewers (optional)

Make it

Recipe

  1. In a large bowl, combine the lemongrass, garlic, ginger, vinegar, calamansi juice, brown sugar, salt, and pepper to make the marinade.
  2. Add the chicken pieces, making sure they are well-coated. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
  3. Prepare your grill with hot coals. Skewer the chicken if desired.
  4. Grill the chicken pieces over medium heat.
  5. Continuously baste the chicken with annatto oil as you turn it every 5 to 7 minutes. This prevents the chicken from drying out and gives it that signature vibrant color.
  6. Grill until the chicken is fully cooked with a nice char on the outside (about 20-30 minutes).
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice and a side of spiced vinegar.
