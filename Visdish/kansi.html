Iloilo and Bacolod

Kansi

Kansi (or Cansi) is an Ilonggo/Bacolodnon comfort food that is best described as a cross between Bulalo (beef bone marrow soup) and Sinigang (sour soup). Originating in the Negros region, its distinct sourness comes from batuan, a sour fruit native to the Visayas. The soup is rich from the slow-cooked beef shank and bone marrow, tangy from the batuan, and slightly thick due to the addition of unripe jackfruit (langka). It is often spiced with annatto for a reddish hue and lemongrass for aroma.

Gather

Ingredients

  • Beef shank with bone marrow
  • Batuan fruit (or tamarind/sinigang mix as an alternative)
  • Lemongrass, pounded
  • Unripe jackfruit (langka), sliced
  • Annatto seeds (soaked in water to extract color)
  • Garlic and onions
  • Finger chilies (siling haba)
  • Fish sauce (patis)
  • Water

Make it

Recipe

  1. In a large pot, cover the beef shanks with water. Bring to a boil and remove the scum.
  2. Add the onions, garlic, and pounded lemongrass. Lower the heat and simmer for 2 to 3 hours until the beef is very tender.
  3. If using fresh batuan, boil them in a separate small pot until soft, mash them to extract the sour juices, and strain the juice into the beef broth. (If using sinigang mix, add it directly).
  4. Add the sliced unripe jackfruit and cook until tender.
  5. Pour in the annatto water to give the broth a slight orange-red color.
  6. Season with fish sauce and add the finger chilies. Let it simmer for another 10 minutes.
  7. Serve piping hot with lots of rice.
