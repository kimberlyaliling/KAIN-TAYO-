La Paz Batchoy

La Paz Batchoy is an iconic Filipino noodle soup that originated in the district of La Paz in Iloilo City. It is a hearty, deeply flavorful dish consisting of fresh miki (egg noodles) submerged in a slow-cooked pork and beef broth, which is traditionally seasoned with guinamos (local shrimp paste). The soup is generously topped with crushed chicharon (pork cracklings), fried garlic, chopped spring onions, and a mix of pork offal (liver, intestines) and beef loin. It is often served with a raw egg cracked on top just before serving.

Gather

Ingredients

  • Fresh miki noodles (egg noodles)
  • Pork bones and beef marrow bones (for broth)
  • Pork belly and pork liver
  • Pork intestines (cleaned thoroughly), optional
  • Guinamos (shrimp paste) or regular bagoong
  • Brown sugar, salt, and pepper
  • Crushed chicharon (pork cracklings)
  • Toasted garlic bits
  • Spring onions, chopped
  • Raw egg (optional, for topping)

Make it

Recipe

  1. In a large pot, boil the pork and beef bones with water for 3 to 4 hours to extract the flavors. Skim the scum periodically.
  2. Add the pork belly and intestines to the pot and cook until tender. Remove the meat once tender, slice them thinly, and set aside.
  3. Season the simmering broth with guinamos, brown sugar, salt, and heavily with black pepper. Let it simmer further.
  4. Briefly blanch the sliced pork liver in the hot broth so it doesn't overcook, then remove and set aside.
  5. Blanch the fresh miki noodles in boiling water for a few seconds, then place them into individual serving bowls.
  6. Arrange the sliced pork, liver, and intestines on top of the noodles.
  7. Pour the boiling hot broth over the noodles and meat.
  8. Garnish heavily with crushed chicharon, toasted garlic, and spring onions. Crack a raw egg on top immediately before serving.
