Famous Dishes

These are the dishes that have captured the hearts and taste buds of people across the Philippines and beyond.

From the bustling streets of Manila to the serene countryside, these dishes represent the rich culinary heritage of the Philippines.

Pork Adobo

Adobo is a popular Filipino comfort food, a savory marinade, and a traditional cooking method derived from the Spanish word adobar (meaning "to marinade").

Sinigang

Sinigang is a popular Filipino sour soup, typically made with tamarind as the main ingredient, giving it its distinctive sour taste.

Dinuguan

Dinuguan is a popular Filipino dish made with pork blood, making it a unique and flavorful comfort food.

Beef Caldereta

Beef Caldereta is a popular Filipino dish made with beef and a rich, savory sauce.

Bistek Tagalog

Bistek Tagalog is a popular Filipino dish made with thinly sliced beef, typically cooked in a savory sauce.

Tinolang Manok

Tinolang Manok is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken cooked in a sour and savory sauce.

Lumpiang Shanghai

Lumpiang Shanghai is a popular Filipino dish made with spring rolls filled with a mixture of ground meat and vegetables.

Tortang Talong

Tortang Talong is a popular Filipino dish made with eggplant cooked in a savory sauce.

Lechong Kawali

Lechong Kawali is a popular Filipino dish made with deep-fried fish cakes.

Daing Bangus

Daing Bangus is a popular Filipino dish made with milkfish cooked in a savory sauce.
