Visayas dishes

The Visayas is the central region of the Philippines, known for its rich cultural heritage and diverse culinary traditions.

Visayas Cuisine

Expect bright, tangy flavors, fresh seafood, grilled specialties, and comforting regional soups.

Western Visayas

Chicken Inasal

Chicken inasal is a signature grilled chicken specialty that originates from the Western Visayas region of the Philippines, most famously associated with Bacolod City.

Central Visayas

Lechon Belly Cebu

Lechon Belly Cebu is a famous dish from Cebu, known for its crispy skin and tender meat, typically served with a sweet and savory sauce.

Western Visayas

La Paz Batchoy

La Paz batchoy is a famous Filipino noodle soup made with pork offal, crushed pork cracklings, and round egg noodles in a rich meat broth.

Western Visayas

Kansi

Kansi is a traditional Filipino dish from the Visayas region, made with a variety of vegetables and meat in a rich, savory sauce.
